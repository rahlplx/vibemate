## 2025-05-18 - JS Map LRU Eviction & Collection Conversion Performance
**Learning:** In TypeScript/JS Map-backed LRU caches, calling `map.get()` without `map.delete(key)` + `map.set(key, val)` leaves insertion order untouched, causing `map.keys().next().value` eviction to act as FIFO instead of LRU. Furthermore, chaining `Array.from(map.entries()).filter().map()` inside collection methods (`keys()`, `values()`, `entries()`) creates 3 intermediate arrays per invocation and evaluates `Date.now()` inside loop predicates, degrading performance by ~3x compared to single-pass `for...of` loops.
**Action:** Always re-insert keys on access to maintain true LRU order in JS Maps, and replace `Array.from().filter().map()` chains with single-pass loops that evaluate timestamps once prior to iteration.
